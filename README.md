# TastyBites Sales & Profitability Analysis

> **Uncovering what drives profit, not just sales.**

## 📊 Project Overview

TastyBites Sales & Profitability Analysis is an interactive Power BI project
designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance,
discount impact, and category-level contribution.

The goal of this analysis is to identify what drives profit, understand
business performance trends, and provide actionable strategies for
profitable growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability
- Track key business KPIs
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends
- Evaluate product-level revenue and profit performance
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profit margin
- Identify the strongest profit-generating categories
- Develop strategies to improve profitability

## 📈 Monthly Performance

Revenue and profit show seasonal patterns, with November and December
performing strongly.

### Key Insight

Q4 is the strongest period, suggesting that inventory, promotions, and
marketing campaigns should be planned ahead of the festive season.

---

## 🍽️ Product Performance

The analysis compares products based on both revenue and profit to identify
products that generate strong sales as well as products that contribute
strongly to profitability.

### Top Profit Contributors

- **Veg Biryani** — 0.96M profit
- **Chicken Biryani** — 0.92M profit
- **Chicken Burger** — 0.91M profit
- **French Fries** — 0.64M profit
- **Veg Sandwich** — 0.63M profit

Veg Biryani is the highest profit-generating product in the analysis.

---

## 💸 Discount vs Profit Margin

The analysis shows a clear relationship between higher discounts and lower
profit margins.

| Discount | Profit Margin |
|---:|---:|
| 0% | 49.15% |
| 25% | 32.18% |

### Key Insight

Profit margin decreases as discounts increase. Maintaining discounts at
moderate levels could help protect profitability.

---

## 🏆 Category Performance

### Meals

Meals are the strongest profit-generating category and contribute more than
two-thirds of total profit.

### Snacks

French Fries and Veg Sandwich are the leading snack products, providing
strong profit contributions.

### Beverages

Cold Coffee is the leading beverage by profit at 0.59M, followed by
Masala Tea at 0.44M.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Meals are the primary profit driver**
   - The Meals category contributes more than two-thirds of total profit.

2. **Q4 is the strongest period**
   - November and December show strong revenue and profit performance.

3. **Discounting affects profitability**
   - Profit margin declines significantly as discount levels increase.

4. **Some products generate strong sales but weaker profit**
   - Veg Burger and Paneer Butter Chicken require pricing and/or cost review.

5. **High-profit products offer promotional opportunities**
   - Veg Biryani, Chicken Biryani, Chicken Burger, French Fries, and
     Veg Sandwich are strong candidates for targeted promotions.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

### Quick Wins

- Promote **Veg Biryani** to increase visibility and sales.
- Promote **Chicken Biryani** and **Chicken Burger** through targeted campaigns.
- Increase focus on high-profit snacks such as **French Fries** and
  **Veg Sandwich**.
- Reduce unnecessary heavy discounts.
- Review pricing and costs for **Veg Burger** and
  **Paneer Butter Chicken**.

### Long-Term Strategies

- Implement price optimization for products with strong sales but lower
  profitability.
- Create profitable bundles combining meals with snacks or beverages.
- Maintain focus on the high-performing Meals category.
- Strengthen the Beverage category, particularly high-potential products
  such as Cold Coffee.
- Prepare inventory, promotions, and campaigns ahead of the Q4 festive season.
